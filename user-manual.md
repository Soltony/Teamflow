# NIB EPMO User Manual

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to the NIB EPMO application! This manual will guide you through the main features of the platform and help you get started with managing your projects.

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1. Logging In

1.  Navigate to the application's login page.
2.  Enter your company-provided phone number and password.
3.  Click "Sign In".
4.  If you are a new user, your default password is `Welcome2PMO`. You will be prompted to change this after your first login.

### 2.2. The Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page you see after logging in. It provides a high-level overview of the entire project portfolio. Here you can see:
- **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Quick stats on project completion, overdue projects, and active blockers.
- **Charts:** Visual breakdowns of projects by status and division.
- **Active Projects Summary:** A list of ongoing projects and their current progress.

## 3. Core Features

### 3.1. Managing Projects

- **Creating a Project (Project Managers & Admins):**
  1.  Navigate to the "Projects" page from the sidebar.
  2.  Click the "Create Project" button.
  3.  Fill in all required details, including name, description, dates, and assign a Project Manager.
  4.  Define at least one milestone with its weight. The total weight of all milestones must equal 100%.
  5.  Click "Create Project" to save.

- **Editing a Project:**
  1.  From the "Projects" page, click on a project card to go to its detail page.
  2.  Click the "Edit Project" button.
  3.  Make your changes and click "Save Changes".

- **Requesting a Deadline Change:**
  1.  While editing a project, change the "End Date".
  2.  When you click "Save Changes", a dialog box will appear.
  3.  Enter a clear reason for the deadline change and click "Submit for Approval". The request will be sent to an administrator.

### 3.2. Managing Milestones & Tasks

- **Viewing Milestones:** Go to the "Milestones" page to see an overview of all milestones across projects, or view them within a specific project's detail page.
- **Adding Tasks (Project Managers & Admins):**
  1.  Navigate to a project's detail page and click "Manage Milestones".
  2.  Find the milestone you want to add a task to and expand it.
  3.  Click the "Add Task" button.
  4.  Fill in the task details, assign users, and set its weight relative to other tasks in that milestone (total must not exceed 100%).
  5.  Click "Add Task".

### 3.3. Updating Your Tasks

- **My Tasks Page:**
  1.  Navigate to the "My Tasks" page from the sidebar. This page shows all tasks assigned to you.
  2.  Tasks are categorized into "Overdue", "Active", and "Accomplished This Week".
  3.  Click on a task to expand it.
  4.  You can post updates, adjust your progress using the slider, and change the task's status from the dropdown menu.
  5.  When you set progress to 100%, the status automatically changes to "Pending Review".

### 3.4. Managing Your Team (Team Leads)

- **Team View Page:**
  1.  Navigate to the "Team View" page.
  2.  This page shows all tasks assigned to members of the teams you lead.
  3.  When a team member sets a task to "Pending Review", it will appear here with "Approve" and "Decline" buttons.
  4.  **Approve:** Marks the task as "DONE".
  5.  **Decline:** Reverts the task to "IN_PROGRESS" and requires you to provide a reason for the rejection, which will be visible to the assignee.

### 3.5. Activity Views

#### 1. Today Page

**Purpose:**
The Today page provides a real-time, daily snapshot of all project activity. It is designed to answer the question:
“What happened today?”

**Details:**

*   Displays all tasks due today.
*   Highlights tasks completed today.
*   Shows tasks that have received progress updates today.

**Use Case:**
This page is most effective for daily stand-up meetings or quick status checks. It enables team members to stay focused on immediate priorities and understand the most urgent activities across all active projects.

#### 2. Weekly Page

**Purpose:**
The Weekly page offers a broader, summarized view of project progress over a selected week—past, present, or future. It helps answer the question:
“What did we accomplish this week?”

**Details:**

*   Provides a weekly summary of project activities.
*   Includes KPI cards showing active projects, updated tasks, and completed tasks during the week.
*   Displays a key planning metric: the number of tasks due in the following week.

**Use Case:**
This page is ideal for weekly review sessions. It helps teams evaluate overall progress, identify trends, and prepare for upcoming work with a clear understanding of weekly performance and workload.

## 4. Governance & Approvals

### 4.1. Timeline Approvals (Admins)

1.  Navigate to the "Timeline Approvals" page from the sidebar.
2.  Here you will see a list of all pending project deadline change requests.
3.  Review the old date, new date, and the reason provided.
4.  Click "Approve" to accept the change and update the project's official deadline.
5.  Click "Reject" to deny the change. You will be prompted to provide a reason. The project's original deadline will remain unchanged.

### 4.2. Payment Approvals (Approvers)

1.  Navigate to the "Payment Approvals" page.
2.  Review the list of pending payments for projects.
3.  Click "Approve" to approve the payment.
4.  Click "Reject" and provide a reason to decline the payment.

## 5. Your Profile

-   Click on your avatar in the bottom-left corner of the sidebar and select "Profile".
-   Here you can update your personal information (email, phone number) and change your password.
-   **Note:** Changing your password will log you out, and you will need to sign in again with your new password.
